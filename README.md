## About the Book

**Statecraft** is an open-access research manual and how-to guide for state-building, written in the narrative non-fiction style of **Malcolm Gladwell**. It is designed to dismantle the common illusion that building a country is a simple, modular task (what we call the **Sandbox Fallacy**).

Through compelling real-world stories, academic research, and concrete policy checklists, the book explores the deep, counter-intuitive systems of power, legitimacy, economics, and sociology that dictate a nation's survival.

---

## Overarching Thesis: Hardware vs. Software

Most failed state-building attempts focus on the **Hardware** of nationhood: drawing physical borders, printing money bills, writing paper constitutions, and amassing standing armies.

This book argues that a state is actually a **Psychological Operating System (Software)**: a web of trust, social coordination, legal fictions, spatial integration, and decentralized data registries. If you master the software, you can build a state anywhere—even in the cloud. If you fail to master the software, you cannot build a country even on the most secure, mineral-rich plot of land.

## Chapter Summaries

### Introduction: The Sandbox Fallacy *(The Danube Anomaly)*
- **The Hook:** Vít Jedlička founds **Liberland** on a Danube riverbank in 2015; Croatian police arrest him on day one.
- **The Pivot:** Montevideo Convention (1933) and Max Weber's monopoly on legitimate force.
- **The Concept:** Westphalian sovereignty, de facto vs. de jure statehood.

### Chapter 1: The Parliament of Elders *(Legitimacy)*
- Somaliland's bottom-up democracy vs. Somalia's $4.2B UN failure; **Isomorphic Mimicry**; the *Guurti* and *Xeer*.

### Chapter 2: The Infrastructure of Trust *(Money)*
- Estonia's 1992 currency crisis; gold reserves; **Currency Board** pegged to the Deutsche Mark.

### Chapter 3: The Apartment Lottery *(Society)*
- Singapore 1964 riots; Gordon Allport's **Contact Hypothesis**; HDB ethnic integration quotas.

### Chapter 4: The Country Without a Shield *(Security)*
- Costa Rica abolishes its military (1948); **Praetorian State**; Rio Treaty peace dividend.

### Chapter 5: The Legal Ghosts *(Law)*
- SMOM vs. Taiwan; **Declarative vs. Constitutive** theories of recognition; sovereignty spectrum.

### Chapter 6: The Digital Fortress *(Digital)*
- Estonia's **X-Road**, e-ID, e-Residency, and Luxembourg **Data Embassies**.

### Chapter 7: The Pump Handle *(Health)*
- John Snow's 1854 cholera map; miasma vs. germ theory; **IHR** and modern biosurveillance.

### Chapter 8: The Human Engine *(Education)*
- Prussian factory-schools vs. Finland and Estonia; **LAYS** human capital metrics.

### Chapter 9: The Extraction Engine *(Fiscal)*
- Whiskey Rebellion; Charles **Tilly** on war and state-making; tax-to-GDP capacity.

### Chapter 10: The Messmer Gambit *(Energy)*
- France's 56-reactor program; **Energiewende**; EROI and grid sovereignty.

### Conclusion: The Architecture of the Accidental State
- Return to Liberland; **Statecraft Matrix**; 18-question Accidental Founder's Diagnostic.

## Repository Structure

```
chapters/
  front_matter.md                 ← Copyright, about, how-to-read, pillars table
  chapter_0_intro.md              ← Introduction
  chapter_1_legitimacy.md …       ← Chapters 1–10
  chapter_11_conclusion.md        ← Conclusion + diagnostic checklist
  back_matter.md                  ← Acknowledgments, references, sources, methods
merge_chapters.py                 ← Merges all parts → book.md
compile_html.py                   ← Builds structured web edition → index.html
compile_pdf.sh                    ← XeLaTeX PDF
compile_epub.sh                   ← EPUB for e-readers
statecraft_book_cover.jpg         ← Cover art
img_*.jpg                         ← Inline infographic charts
.github/workflows/deploy.yml       ← CI: merge → HTML → GitHub Pages
.github/workflows/release.yml     ← CD: tag → PDF + EPUB + book.md → Release
```

> **Note:** `book.md`, `statecraft_manual.pdf`, and `statecraft.epub` are generated artifacts not committed to the repo.

**Build locally:**

```bash
python3 merge_chapters.py
python3 compile_html.py          # → index.html (web)
bash compile_pdf.sh              # → statecraft_manual.pdf
bash compile_epub.sh             # → statecraft.epub
```
