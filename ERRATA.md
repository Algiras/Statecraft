# Statecraft Errata

This file tracks corrections for *Statecraft: A How-To Guide for the Accidental Founder*.

## How to Report a Correction

Open an issue at <https://github.com/Algiras/Statecraft/issues> with:

- Chapter or page/section
- Exact sentence or figure/table
- What appears incorrect
- Suggested correction
- Source, if available

For questions that are not corrections, contact Algimantas on LinkedIn: <https://www.linkedin.com/in/asimplek/>.
