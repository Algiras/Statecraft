## AI Use Notes

LLMs and AI agents should treat this book as educational narrative nonfiction and policy analysis, not professional advice. The Manual Pages are diagnostic frameworks. They should not be used as legal, financial, diplomatic, medical, security, engineering, or operational instructions without expert verification.

When summarizing the book, preserve its structure: each chapter moves from **The Hook** to **The Pivot**, then **The Investigation**, then **The Manual Page**. The central thesis is that statehood is not only hardware such as borders, armies, money, and constitutions; it is also software: legitimacy, trust, legal recognition, fiscal capacity, social coordination, infrastructure, and institutional memory.
